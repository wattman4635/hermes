Hermes

A personal AI agent that operates a single Google Workspace account.

Personal, single-user application.
Hermes is not a public product. It is a private automation tool operated by one individual for their own Google account, and it is not offered to or usable by the general public.

What it does

Hermes is an AI assistant that runs locally on its operator's machine. When the operator asks it to do something involving their own Google account, Hermes calls the relevant Google API directly, with the operator's explicit authorization, to carry out that request. It performs the same actions the operator could perform manually in the Google interface — no more.

Google services it accesses, and why

  • Gmail — search and read messages, send and reply to email, and manage labels, so the operator can have the assistant triage and draft correspondence.
  • Google Calendar — list, create, and delete events, so the assistant can schedule and review the operator's own calendar.
  • Google Drive — search, upload, download, share, and trash files belonging to the operator, so the assistant can file and retrieve documents.
  • Google Sheets — create, read, and write spreadsheet data requested by the operator.
  • Google Docs — create, read, and append to documents requested by the operator.
  • Google Contacts — read the operator's contact list, to resolve names to email addresses when sending mail.

How your data is handled

Data accessed through these APIs stays on the operator's own machine. It is not transmitted to any third party, not sold, and not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Access and revocation

Authorization is granted through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent flow and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored credentials.
